# Qwen3 V3 audit and practical protocol recommendation

The V3 result does not justify the more elaborate review schema. Independent scoring gives 34 valid responses, 28/40 strictly correct, two false accepts and nine exclusions among the 24 supported references. The raw verdict-only diagnostic is 34/40, compared with 38/40 for Qwen3 V1. These are fixed purposive training cases, not a general factual reliability estimate.

**Request construction check:** all 40 V3 user payloads have identical game metadata, candidate claims, original candidate citations and source context to V1 after removing the output schema. No source lines or candidate citation IDs were lost in the variant builder. All 40 responses ended with `stop`; final output lengths were 823–2,064 tokens, so the observed failures are not truncated JSON. Batch generation took 178.338 seconds, counted once.

**Observed failure mechanism:** the extra six-attribute matrix creates opportunities for unsupported intermediate assertions and enum errors. Three responses use invalid assessments (`complete`, `supported`, `complete_citation`). Three correctly support the underlying fact but invent an incomplete-citation matrix that conflicts with that verdict. Examples include a candidate citation `[3,8,10]` described as omitting line 3, and `[1,2,4,7,8]` described as omitting line 8. Valid responses also assert that `[13,15,19,20,24]` omits line 19 and `[5,6,7]` omits line 7. These are model-generated set-containment errors; the original request contains the lines.

V3 also rejects Morant's current-season season-ending injury as merely historical, and requires extra injury-report lines to support Durant's appropriate caveat that prior absences do not establish tonight's status. The two semantic false accepts persist from V1: the Robinson/Herro player-card mix-up and the Miami claim whose original citation omits the sentence continuation. Thus the schema added false exclusions without resolving the known hard semantic errors. Output/prompt burden and the repeated emphasis on citation completeness are plausible contributors; these runs do not isolate a causal mechanism or prove that any single wording caused the regression.

A bounded next step is to return the production review interface to the compact V1-style assessment, preserving full original outcome-blind request evidence, source headings and canonical line references. Keep a concise source reading, verdict, reason and required evidence references for each claim; do not require six repetitive enum-valued comparisons. Code should check source identity, line existence and whether reviewer-nominated required lines are contained in the original candidate citation set. Extra context lines are not a missing-citation error. A reviewer must still decide which lines are actually necessary and whether they entail the claim; mechanical containment cannot certify those judgments or resolve the player-card error.

Use the three already selected training games as the next gate, not another benchmark variant. Compare complete candidates, reviewer rejections and surviving reports against the 29 source-only expectations. Keep every rejection and original candidate. If a reviewer rejects a fact because a line is supposedly absent, expose the exact original/required line sets for source-based adjudication rather than deleting the fact or retrying until acceptance. If it confuses historical and current season-ending status, inspect the source date/season and surrounding section. Any repair must be a new audited artifact; do not silently override the existing verdict.

Qwen3 remains the strongest tested candidate under the simpler support task, so keep it as the initial gold-writing candidate. Existing 72B may provide a separate review signal for the three-game experiment, but its V3 errors do not justify automatically stripping evidence or treating acceptance as certification. Before corpus-scale production, inspect those complete reports and decide whether its actual disputes are manageable or a different historically eligible reviewer is necessary. Do not choose a new model merely from parameter count, and do not use model self-agreement as an independent check. Full production quality is not established yet.
