BUCK SHOTS
•
The 56th season of Bucks basketball tips off on Thursday night as the Bucks lift the lid on their 2023-
24 season against the 76ers at Fiserv Forum. Milwaukee is opening its season at home for just the
second time in the last seven seasons, with the Bucks winning five of their last six season openers.
• It's the regular season debut for new Bucks head coach Adrian Griffin, who takes over as the 17th
head coach in franchise history. Griffin heads a Milwaukee team that went an NBA-best 58-24 (.707)
last season, which marked the third time in the last five seasons the Bucks have owned the top
record in the NBA. Since 2018-19, no team has a better record than the Bucks, who have gone 271-
120 (.693) in that span.
Two major moves highlighted the Bucks' offseason as the team acquired 7x All-Star and 7x All-NBA
honoree Damian Lillard prior to training camp before re-signing 2x MVP Giannis Antetokounmpo to a
multi-year extension on Monday. Milwaukee's new duo ranked 3rd and 5th, respectively, in the NBA in
scoring last season, with Lillard averaging a career-high 32.2 ppg and Antetokounmpo averaging a
career-high 31.1 ppg. Teaming up this season, Antetokounmpo and Lillard are just the second pair of
teammates in NBA history to have both averaged 30+ ppg in the previous season.
The Bucks also re-signed two major pieces of their core in free agency, bringing back Brook Lopez,
who was a finalist for Defensive Player of the Year last season, along with 3x All-Star Khris Middleton.
Lopez, who is entering his 6th season with the Bucks, blocked a league-high 193 shots last season,
while Middleton, the franchise's all-time leader in 3FGM and 7th on the franchise scoring list, is
entering his 11th season with the Bucks.
Bucks big man Bobby Portis Jr.put together a Sixth Man of the Year worthy campaign in 2022-23. A
finalist for the award last season, Portis Jr. averaged 14.1 points and a career-high 9.6 rebounds per
game last season, coming off the bench in 48 of his 70 games played. Overall, he ranked 9th in the
NBA in double-doubles with 38, with a league-high 23 of those coming off the bench.
